# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Exaggerated-real-physics redo (the house style, now written up in the guide). Spine: new `explosion` (1/t flash, Sedov-Taylor shockwave r ~ t^0.4, fireball that balloons the same way while cooling white -> orange -> smoke and rising, debris with air drag stretched along its speed, embers, dust ring; `ae_hint` for a volumetric fireball) and `shine` (instant bloom with a 1/t tail, counter-turning ray stars, anamorphic streak, chromatic halo rings, twinkles; `pulse` loops it); `portal` has differential rotation (inner 3x faster) and Keplerian infall for its specks, and the flashes pump the vortex; `water_splash` gains a crown, a central jet that pinches off a drop, sqrt(t) capillary rings and landing ripples; `frost` tips race then slow, branches stop on contact, a glowing growth front, a late wave of needles. After Effects: `AEFX.loopify` (render a drifting source twice as long and crossfade it into a seamless loop that still moves); `fire` rises at `rise` px/s with fast licks over the slow body, flickers at 11 Hz, white-hot core, hard `alpha_cut`; `smoke_puff` bursts (exponent `burst`), rises, rolls as it slows, is lit from above (`shade`); `smoke_haze` convects upward with a parallax deep layer and a swirling convection cell; `lightning` has a stepped leader that grows across, return-stroke flash as the channel's own air-glow, branches dying first, `restrikes`. 7 new tests.

- Ice and water elements (`claude_spine/fx_elements.py`, Spine alone): `frost` (ferns grow across a box or from the centre like a snowflake, as a generated flipbook with 60-degree branching and a frosted film; glow, glints; event `fx_frost_done`), `icicles` (grow down from the top edge under a frost crust; glints run down, drops swell and fall), `ice_shatter` (procedural ice block, Voronoi crack lines, shards fly and fall; mist, ring; events `fx_ice_crack` / `fx_ice_shatter`), `bubbles` (rise, wobble, squish, pop; loops) and `water_splash` (droplets on parabolas turned and stretched along their speed, ripple rings, mist; event `fx_splash`). AE template `caustics` (shimmering underwater light webs, seamless loop, additive). Guide gains an ice/water section. 5 new tests.

- More from the next references: `meteor_trace` (bright head + comet tail racing around a rounded-rect frame of any size, tail bends round the corners, sparks, loops exactly) and `projectile` (shot on an arc to a target with a comet tail and sparks, then impact flash, ring and starburst; events `fx_projectile_launch` / `fx_projectile_hit`). Tails are strand meshes whose rows sit on the path at a lag and rotate to the tangent, angles unwrapped. Realistic smoke: AE templates `smoke_puff` (billow that bursts, rolls and erodes) and `smoke_haze` (looping haze filling a box); `puff` gains `lobes=False` (flash + ring + `ae_hint` for the AE smoke). `fire` gains `edge_fade` (off by default) so narrow tongues never show the comp border; one tongue cloned along a frame with per-clone sequence index gives a flame border. Frame underglows (electric_frame, meteor_trace) are now 9-slices, so long bars stay even. Every hybrid returns `ae_hint`.

- Two more `fx_recipe` recipes from the next reference clip, Spine alone: `puff` (flash, cartoon cloud lobes burst outward around a core puff, swell and thin out, soft ring; blend normal or additive; event `fx_puff`) and `smoke_glow` (rising haze filling a box with a flame-coloured glow at its base; loops exactly). `cell_glow` gains `fill_color` / `fill_alpha` (a translucent warm fill instead of the dark one). Guide shows how to mix several recipes into one clip (gold cell + haze + puff). Procedural cloud textures are round overlapping discs (a sine-wobbled radius made spiky crumpled-paper shapes).

- `art=` on every `fx_recipe`: use your own pictures instead of the generated ones and keep all the motion (timing, flashes, events). `art={role: spec}` where the roles come from the listing (`art_roles`; e.g. crosshair: reticle, glow, ring, flash) and a spec is a PNG, a PSD layer (`file.psd#Layer` or `file.psd#Group/Layer`, cut at its own bounding box) or `{path, blend, scale, slice, px, anchor}`. The recipe keeps its slot width and your art keeps its aspect; wisp/beam/reflection art anchors at its base edge; 9-slice art takes `slice` (corner px) and `px` (units per px); bundles take art per member. Unknown roles, missing files and missing PSD layers are clear errors. 8 new tests (PNG, PSD layer with nested group, blend/scale, anchors, 9-slice, bundles).

- Four more `fx_recipe` recipes, all Spine alone: `crosshair` (reticle drops in spinning, locks on with flash and shock ring, hovers, recoils when it fires; events `fx_crosshair_lock` / `fx_crosshair_hit`), `hit_burst` (starburst, core, two shock rings, sparks; event `fx_hit`), `lock_on` (volley: crosshairs lock onto any number of targets in turn, each followed by a hit burst) and `cell_glow` (glowing cell frames with a twinkling starfield that pop with a flash, burst ring and sparkles; the frame and a darkening fill are a 9-slice mesh pair on four shared corner bones, so any width/height keeps sharp corners and the game can resize by moving the corners). Guide gains a resize/recolour section for glowing frames.

- Three more `fx_recipe` recipes, from new reference clips: `light_beam` (gold, ribbon and blue beams: column glow, filaments, weaving ribbon strands built as weighted meshes with one bone per row so they cost no deform keys, dust and glints; loops exactly), `portal` (swirling disc, counter-rotating spiral arms, orbiting specks, comet streaks, flashes) and `electric_frame` (underglow + sparks). The last two are hybrids: their plasma ring / lightning line is After Effects, via two new templates `portal_ring` and `electric_frame` (turbulent displacement + fractal-noise plasma, seamless loops) and `ae_fx_to_spine`; each recipe returns a `ring_hint` with the exact arguments. Guide extended with the hybrid workflow, a Spine-vs-AE table (crosshairs, hit bursts, coin pops, smoke) and the traps (SDF glow fill, AE noise clipping, deform keys vs the mobile budget).

- `fx_recipe` (new tool) + `claude_spine/fx_recipes.py`: seven authored FX layers lifted from a real reference clip (a lotus blooming out of a magic book) as reusable, parameterised recipes: `rune_ring`, `burst_flare`, `rim_wisps`, `bloom_aura`, `floor_glow`, `fireflies`, `twinkles`, plus `magic_reveal` (all seven timed like the clip, 13.2 s). Procedural textures, additive only (one draw call), one group bone per recipe so it moves, recolours (`color=`), resizes (`scale=`) and retimes as one piece, and merges into any animation with `into=`. `fx_recipe` with no recipe lists them with options and defaults; `recipe="guide"` returns the guide (use / fork / mix / how they were made / traps), mirrored in `docs/FX_RECIPES.md`. Each recipe fires an `fx_<recipe>` event. 28 new tests (every recipe builds, validates and plays in the spine-core runtime; shared arguments; respawn-at-alpha-0 for looping motes; bundle budget = 1 draw call).

- `ae_template` (26th tool) + `claude_spine/ae_templates/`: eight parameterised After Effects FX templates as ExtendScript (glow_pulse, shockwave, sparkle, relief_shimmer, fire, lightning, burst, splash). `ae_template` returns a script; running it in AE builds one ready-to-render comp, which goes straight to `ae_fx_to_spine`. Built-in AE effects only, so scripts render anywhere with `aerender`. Verified end to end (template → AE → save → aerender → Spine → spine-core runtime).

- `ae_fx_to_spine` (25th tool): After Effects → Spine bridge. Renders a saved `.aep` comp headless with `aerender` (never touches the project open in AE), reads AE's premultiplied TIFF alpha, trims empty frames, writes straight-alpha sprites to `images/ae/` and plays them as a Spine sequence at the comp's own speed. `hit_ae` + `hit_at` land an AE moment on a Spine one; `fit_duration` stretches a sequence to a loop length; `max_frames`/`max_size` shrink it. Frames already on disk work too (`frames_dir` + `fps`). Reproduces the coin project's hand-made fire flipbook byte for byte. 11 new tests.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-05

First release of the restructured fork of egorfedorov/spine-mcp.

- Typed Spine 4.2 IR (Pydantic) with exact runtime defaults and pass-through of unknown fields; bone-index remapping for weighted vertices.
- Contour meshes: padded trace → DP simplify (never clips art) → joint-aware refinement → verified conforming Delaunay with an exact CDT fallback.
- Bone-heat weights (cotangent Laplacian, visibility-aware heat), ≤4 influences, exact normalisation.
- IK, Spine 4.2 physics presets, transform constraints, one-call strand rigs, 2.5D turn rig.
- Symbol animation contract + juice presets on inserted juice bones; FX presets with procedural textures; Spine events for SFX/particles/rollups.
- QA: structural validation, spine-core runtime load + playback check, mobile budget with draw-call estimate, runtime-driven GIF previews.
- MaxRects atlas packer (whitespace strip, PMA, sequence-safe region names).
- 24 MCP tools; 369 tests including mesh fuzzing and a Spine editor round-trip.
