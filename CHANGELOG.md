## Unreleased

- AE template `ripple_glow`: soft smoky ripple glow, rebuilt from a slot clip of a charged multiplier orb and reworked from the artist's notes. Waves form small at the centre (or at the rim, `origin`), expand outward through a smoky rim and fade, and the next one forms in the middle. `look=smoke` (default) draws each wave as evolving fractal-noise smoke inside a torn, blurred ring (luma matte) with a faint glow; `look=line` draws crisp rings. Every wave in the loop is different (seeded distortion, rotation, squash, size, timing), each trails `echo` rings, and the halo swells as a wave crosses the rim. The colour is bright, not white (saturated blue to bright cyan). Other options: `softness`, `inner`, `rim`, and `shape` circle or rect (a rect only flips). It is built in grey and coloured once, so it imports tintable: one frame set played blue, purple and gold through exact `LIGHT/DARK` pairs (a single hue tint lands off-colour when the fitted ends differ in hue). Rendered in AE 26.5: 0% near-white pixels and a loop seam of 1.4/255. It plays in the spine-core runtime over the clip, and Spine 4.2.43 imports it with zero repairs. The clip-to-spine `render.py` starter now draws two-colour tint, so tintable imports no longer preview grey. 1 test.
- `render_comp` retries aerender (up to 3 runs) when its log comes back without the comp's settings: back-to-back renders on AE 26.5 sometimes write every frame but print no frame rate, which failed `ae_quick_look` and the imports. Found building six glow states on the coin. 1 test.
